# ReconPlus

![preview image](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/meowl492/reconplus/refs/heads/main/ReconPlus_zem4cYdMrd.png)

**ReconPlus** is a feature-rich, high-performance web and desktop client built to enhance your discovery, messaging, and social experience. Designed with a modern, responsive user interface, ReconPlus brings intuitive navigation, deep customization, real-time interactivity, and enhanced privacy controls right to your screen.

---

## Features Highlight

### Advanced Search & Profile Grid
* **Dense Profile Card Layout:** Browse profiles efficiently in a clean, distance-sorted visual grid.
* **Instant Side-Panel Preview:** Inspect detailed member profiles, photos, and bio information instantly without losing your place or reloading the page.
* **Comprehensive Filter Drawer:** Refine search criteria with granular controls:
  * Age & Distance range sliders
  * Multi-select lookups for interests, roles, positions, body types, body hair, and safety preferences
  * Quick toggle switches for *Online Now*, *New Members*, and *Has Photos*
* **Proximity & Name Search:** Real-time search by member name combined with precise location-based proximity filtering.
* **Interactive Active Filter Chips:** Easily review and clear applied filters on the fly.

### Real-Time Messaging & Chat
* **Split-Panel Workspace:** Enjoy a smooth chat workflow with an interactive conversation drawer on the left and dedicated active message view on the right.
* **Instant Real-Time Sync:** Receive live incoming messages, typing notifications, and instant unread count updates seamlessly.
* **Rich Media Attachments:** Inline image rendering within message bubbles paired with a full-screen preview lightbox viewer.
* **Media Attachment Picker:** Quickly attach and send uploaded photos directly from your media gallery.
* **Conversation Management:** Clear read indicators, hide/delete chat threads, and jump straight into existing messaging history.

### Activity Hub & Notifications
* **Visitors & Views Log:** Keep track of who viewed your profile and who you’ve interacted with.
* **Real-Time Notification Feed:** Stay up to date with activity, reactions, and interaction logs.
* **Social Requests Management:** Easily review, accept, or decline incoming connection and friend requests in one organized view.
* **Followed Members Home Feed:** Dedicated activity stream highlighting updates and interactions from members you follow.

### Connections & Social Graph
* **Interactive Social Overview:** Quickly review Followers, Followings, and Friends counts directly from member profiles.
* **Social Relations Dashboard:** Multi-tab modal interface allowing smooth navigation between your connections.
* **Relationship Management:** Follow/unfollow, send or respond to friend requests, and control mutual connection visibility.

### Profile Customization & Privacy
* **Full Profile Editing:** In-app controls for personalizing your profile handle, bio details, bio parameters, traits, and selected interests.
* **Granular Privacy Controls:** Manage visibility settings for profile visits, push notifications, and email alerts for interactions.
* **Metric & Imperial Preferences:** Toggle between kilometers/miles and height measurements (cm/ft-in) across the app.
* **Blocked User Management:** Searchable block list to review and unblock profiles easily.

## User Experience & Design
* **Modern Aesthetic:** Deep dark mode interface with vibrant accent highlights and glassmorphic UI elements.
* **Cross-Platform Readiness:** Designed for seamless responsiveness across web browsers and desktop platforms.
* **Smooth Animations:** Micro-interactions, hover effects, and fast panel transitions for an ultra-fluid application feel.
